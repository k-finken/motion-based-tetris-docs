Motion-Based Tetris is a Python desktop application that replaces your keyboard with hand gestures. It uses **MediaPipe** and **CVZone** to track your hands through your webcam in real time, identifies which fingers are extended, and maps those gestures to keyboard inputs sent directly to the browser-based Tetris game at [tetris.com](https://tetris.com/play-tetris). The result is a fully hands-free Tetris experience where moving left, moving right, rotating, and hard-dropping all happen with a simple gesture.

## Requirements

Before running Motion-Based Tetris, make sure you have the following:

- **Python 3.x** installed on your machine
- **A webcam** — built-in laptop camera or any external USB webcam
- **A modern browser** with [tetris.com](https://tetris.com/play-tetris) open and focused
- **Windows OS** — required for `pydirectinput`, which handles low-level keyboard simulation

<Note>
  Motion-Based Tetris controls the browser-based Tetris game at tetris.com. You must have that page open and focused in your browser for the gestures to work.
</Note>
